How to Accept

  1. If FanVest is not installed, tap Download on the App Store.
  2. After installing, return to the original conversation in Messages.
  3. Tap the original FanVest invitation again.
  4. Sign in with Apple for Friends, review the invitation, and choose Accept or Not Now.

If the Invitation Is Not Available

FanVest invitations are one-time links and can expire or be cancelled. If FanVest says the invitation is invalid, already used, expired, or cancelled, ask the sender to create a new invitation. This page does not check the invitation with FanVest servers and does not reveal who sent it.
